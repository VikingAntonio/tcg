# Vikingdev TCG - Album Cover Improvements

## 1. Vault (Standard) Style
Synchronized with inversiones.html header.
- Background: Pure black (#000)
- Typography: 3rem font-size, 900 weight, -0.04em letter-spacing.
- Subtitle: Added "Portfolio Management" for 100% parity.

## 2. Cosmic (Galaxy) Style
Complete rebuild of the deep space simulation.
- Multiple Layers: 4 independent layers of box-shadow particles.
- Particle Density: ~900 randomized particles across different sizes and colors (white, pastel blue, pastel pink, pastel yellow).
- Animations: Used prime-number durations (127s, 79s, 53s, 41s) for the layers to ensure the pattern never repeats in a recognizable way.
- Movement: Combinations of drift, rotation, and skew for a truly random liquid space feel.

## 3. Holographic Style
Improved liquid pastel effect.
- Colors: Vibrant saturated pastels (FFB7B2, B5EAD7, C7CEEA, E2F0CB, FFDAC1).
- Overlapping Animations: 'liquid-holographic-flow' (31s) and 'liquid-holographic-metaballs' (23s) with different speeds and directions.
- Contrast: Title font changed to pure black (#000) with a white text-shadow.

## 4. Luxury, Marble, Velvet
Added high-density particle clouds tailored to each style.
- Luxury: High-density gold dust (#D4AF37).
- Marble: Mineral flecks (#888) with randomized drift.
- Velvet: Bright white sparks (#fff) with breathing movement.

All styles have been cleared of previous "grey arc" artifacts to ensure the deepest black backgrounds requested.
